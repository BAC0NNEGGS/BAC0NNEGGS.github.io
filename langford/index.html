About The Langford

A New Waterfront Address for Newport News

The Langford pairs contemporary architecture with an unmatched setting — where tidewater views meet an easy commute to the region's largest employers.

Most homes are planned around water views, natural light, and quiet, durable finishes — walk-in closets, in-unit laundry, and private balconies throughout. Building services like valet trash concierge and a staffed community room round out daily life.
